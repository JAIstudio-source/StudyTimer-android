# StudyTimer 3.3.6 (versionCode 50)

### What's New in Version 3.3.6 🚀
- **Streak Animation & Goal Rule Fixes**: Streak celebration correctly respects "Count streak only on goal hit" setting and smoothly chains with daily goal celebrations.
- **Leaderboard Streak Accuracy**: Resolved stale streaks when a streak breaks; properly calculates and syncs active/broken streaks in real time.
- **Leaderboard Alignment & Top Ranks**: Centered text and stats cards in student profile dialogs; aligned rank numbers, avatars, tags, and study durations across leaderboard lists.
- **UI/UX & Accessibility Polish**: Expanded touch targets and added TalkBack accessibility across bottom sheets, dialogs, and setting toggles.

---

## Compatibility
- Android 9 (API 28) through Android 16 (API 36)

## Installation
Install `StudyTimer-release.apk` (v3.3.6). Updating preserves all your study logs, streaks, and settings.
